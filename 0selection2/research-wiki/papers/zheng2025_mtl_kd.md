# One-line thesis
Distill a pool of RL specialists into one heavy-decoder model; label-free; 6 seen + 10 unseen tasks up to 1000 nodes.

## Relevance to This Project
Alternative paradigm to selection: fuse specialists via KD instead of routing at inference. Useful baseline.
