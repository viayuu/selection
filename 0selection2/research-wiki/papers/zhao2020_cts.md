# One-line thesis
First end-to-end deep TSP solver selector (CNN on image-encoded instances); pre-dates NSS by 4 years.
## Relevance
Must-cite prior art for any deep selector framing; shows image-based encoding works for TSP but is awkward for ATSP/CVRP — motivates graph-based unification.
