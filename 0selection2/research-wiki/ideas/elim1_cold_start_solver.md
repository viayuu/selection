# Why eliminated
Cold-start claim cannot be convincingly proven without *actually* adding unseen solvers to the pool — we do not have that. Dot-product-based new-solver embedding is standard recommender machinery, reviewer would dismiss as weak evidence. Defer to future work if a new solver is integrated mid-project.
